ABOUT JIUKU

Equipment, software and spaces—working together to create fun

JiuKu develops and manufactures unattended amusement equipment, connecting the product lifecycle with software, IoT and operating support.

WHO WE ARE

Amusement technology, rooted in Panyu, Guangzhou

Founded in 2014, Guangzhou JiuKu Amusement Technology began with unattended entertainment equipment such as claw machines. We continue to expand our capabilities across hardware development, appearance and function customization, digital management and venue delivery.

We look beyond equipment delivery to the experience, operating efficiency and long-term value each product creates in a real venue.
